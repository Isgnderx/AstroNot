Supported coordinate systems

RA and Dec are computed from the image header, never from pixel counts. Supported: the primary WCS with RA---TAN and DEC--TAN, with or without -SIP distortion (order up to 9), in ICRS and degrees, using PCi_j with CDELTi or CDi_j. CRPIX may be negative or outside the image. Other projections, frames, legacy CROTA keywords and alternate WCS are reported as unavailable. Pixel values shown are calibrated values, independent of the display contrast.

Pixel (x, y) uses the FITS convention: the center of the first pixel is (1, 1) and y increases upward.
