Forced Spectrophotometry

Measure SPHEREx spectra by forced ePSF photometry on every spectral image covering a position, read directly from the IRSA archive — the IRSA Spectrophotometry Tool's method, in your browser.

For aperture photometry of calibrated images you already have, use Spectrophotometry.

Frames

Import one or more IRSA SIA result files (CSV) saved from Observation Search: every SPHEREx level 2 image in them is a frame, read from the IRSA archive copy on Amazon S3 a few kilobytes at a time. You can also choose SPHEREx level 2 FITS files on this device; a file needs its EPSF extension (QR3, DR1 and later) to be measured. A frame listed twice is measured once.

Targets

Up to 20 sky positions (ICRS). Every target is fitted on every frame it falls on; targets close together are fitted jointly. A target is a point source unless you switch on its Galaxy model (a Sérsic profile convolved with the ePSF; an effective radius below 1 arcsec is treated as a point source, as IRSA does). A targets CSV has the columns ra, dec and optionally name, sersic_n, re_arcsec, axis_ratio, pa_deg, in degrees.

    Filters and parameters

    Filters choose which loaded frames are measured (MJD is the frame start time) and which spectral points are kept (wavelength in μm). Leave a bound empty for no limit. The defaults are spxphot's.

    Results

    Load frames and enter a target, then press Measure.

    One row per target and frame, sorted by label and wavelength until you sort by a column. Wavelength and width in μm, flux in μJy, background in MJy/sr, positions in full-frame pixels. Select a row with Enter or a click.

    Downloads

    The IRSA-format CSV has spxphot's 19 columns with the IRSA table's names (label, ra, dec, lambda, lambda_width, flux, flux_err, flux_err_tractor, flags, fit_ql, flux_bkg, lvf_id, det_id, deep_flg, mjd, data_collection, x_image, y_image, n_pix). The Spectrum Lab CSV has the Spectrum Lab columns (wavelength in μm, flux in μJy) with method forced-epsf.
